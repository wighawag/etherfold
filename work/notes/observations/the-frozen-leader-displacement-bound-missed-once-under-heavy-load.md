# The FROZEN-leader case missed its displacement bound once under heavy load

Observed 2026-09-28 while building `the-tab-lease-suites-stop-flaking`. In one of 3000 runs of `docs/spikes/the-frozen-leader-takeover-flake/repro.sh` (`PARALLEL=30 BURNERS=30`, load average about 100 to 120 on 32 cores), the FROZEN case in `packages/browser/test/aVisibleTabTakesTheLeaseFromABackgroundedLeader.test.ts` failed at `expect(Date.now() - started).toBeLessThan(SETTLE_MS + 1500)` with 1738 ms, before the advance the task fixed. A second run of 1500 under the same load, timing the case, measured a worst of 210 ms (median about 170 ms, reader `init` at most 47 ms of it), and a further clean 3000 runs failed nothing, so it reads as a whole-process stall under CPU starvation rather than a takeover window; not diagnosed further, and the bound was deliberately not loosened. If it shows in CI, time `init`, the settle timer and the steal separately before touching the bound.
