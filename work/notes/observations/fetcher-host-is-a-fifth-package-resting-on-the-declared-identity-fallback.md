2026-09-18 — Noticed while GROUNDING `the-last-four-packages-stop-resting-on-the-declared-fallback` against the current tree, which is that task's first acceptance criterion. The probe from `work/notes/observations/four-more-packages-still-rest-on-the-declared-identity-fallback.md` was re-run across the WHOLE tree (both halves of the fallback made to throw in `dist/`), and it reproduced that note's four counts exactly — `processor-sqlite` 16, `conformance-workload-stratagems` 14, `browser` 10, `platforms/nodejs-fetcher` 6 — plus **two failures in `packages/fetcher-host`, which that note listed nowhere**, neither among the four nor among the packages it reported green.

The site is `packages/fetcher-host/test/harness.ts`, lines 199–200: `new VersionedStateEventProcessor(db, entityProcessor)` and `new StreamBuilder(processor, SOURCE, {stream: {finality: FINALITY}})`, neither given an identity. It is the same shape, line for line, as `platforms/nodejs-fetcher/test/deployment.ts`, which that task migrated; the two harnesses are siblings (one drives the receiver in-process, the other over a real socket) and only one of them was measured.

It is worth knowing HOW it hides, because it is not the ordinary case: the two failures do not surface as the probe's error. They are `the host holds nothing that outlives it > sends a batch through the real wire without either half being mocked` and `> stops when its stream config does not match the receiver, before fetching a log`, and both report `expected undefined to match object {fromBlock: 100, toBlock: 120}`. The throw is swallowed somewhere on the push path and reaches the assertion as a missing result, so a reader skimming a probe run for `PROBE:` in the output would conclude this package was green.

Left deliberately unfixed: `the-last-four-packages-stop-resting-on-the-declared-fallback` fences its edits to four named packages and instructs that a fifth found by the re-run be RECORDED rather than absorbed, so the scope stays reviewable. Unverified beyond the measurement: I did not triage the two cases into remainder or witness. Their subject is the fetch host's own policy (what it pushes, and that it stops on a stream-config mismatch), which reads like a remainder needing a name, but that is a judgement for whoever owns the fix — `the-declared-version-and-the-drift-report-are-deleted` will otherwise meet it.
